Straight answers

Questions owners ask us.

If yours is not here, message us. A person replies.

Do you have case studies?

Not yet. Elitrove is a new agency, and we will not show you made-up numbers or borrowed testimonials. What we can show is the work: the ads, the videos and this site. That is why it starts with a call, so you can judge us before you spend anything.

How much ad budget do I need?

Most local campaigns can start at around $10 to $20 a day. What matters more than size is focus: one offer, one service area. You pay the ad spend straight to Meta.

What should a lead cost?

Home-service leads from Meta often land somewhere around $26 to $50. That is a general industry range, not our result and not a promise. Your offer, your area and how fast you reply all move it.

What does it cost to work with you?

A flat monthly fee from $500, depending on your trade and the size of your jobs. You get your exact price on the call, before any work begins. Ad spend is separate and goes straight to Meta.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. We start, look at the results together after two weeks, and decide from there.

What do you need from me?

Partner access to your ad account in Meta Business settings, photos or short phone clips of real jobs, your service area, the jobs you want more of, and someone who answers new leads quickly.

Is my ad account safe with you?

You never share a password. You add us as a partner in Meta Business settings, and you can remove us there at any time. The ad account and the page stay yours. This site itself sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties.

Which businesses do you work with?

Local service businesses: roofers, AC and heating, electricians, plumbers, pressure washing, tree service, fencing, painting, detailing, cleaning and landscaping. If homeowners in your area hire you, the same setup fits.
